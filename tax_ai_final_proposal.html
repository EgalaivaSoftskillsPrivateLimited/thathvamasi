1. What Are We Building?

A private AI system for Income Tax, GST, TDS, Accounting and Compliance work that helps finance and tax professionals complete actual day-to-day work — not just answer questions.

The system reads, validates and processes:
  • PDF invoices, Excel registers, Tally/ERP exports
  • GSTR-2B JSON, Form 26AS, AIS, TIS downloads
  • Bank statements, salary registers, challan data
  • Previous ITRs, Form 16, investment proofs
The system produces:
  • GSTR-1 working + portal-ready JSON
  • GSTR-2B reconciliation + ITC eligibility
  • GSTR-3B computation + net tax payable
  • TDS computation + FVU data
  • ITR working + tax computation
  • Professional Excel / PDF / Word reports
Instead of an employee doing every step manually, the AI does the repetitive preparation and checking — the professional reviews exceptions and approves.

5. GSTR-2B — ITC Reconciliation

What it is: Auto-drafted ITC statement generated by the GST portal on the 14th of every month. Shows all purchase invoices suppliers have filed. Company can only claim ITC that appears in GSTR-2B.

The core problem: GSTR-2B must be matched against the company's own purchase register line by line. Mismatches mean ITC is over-claimed (audit risk) or under-claimed (cash lost). Done manually in VLOOKUP across thousands of rows.

Manual process today (4–5 hrs):

1
Download GSTR-2B JSON/Excel from portal (14th of month)
2
Get purchase register from client — Tally / ERP / manual Excel
3
Match invoice by invoice — GSTIN + Invoice No + Date + Amount (VLOOKUP)
4
Identify: In 2B not in books / In books not in 2B / Amount diff / GSTIN diff
5
Apply Section 17(5) blocked credits — exclude personal use, vehicles, food
6
Prepare final ITC eligibility working for GSTR-3B

6. GSTR-3B — Summary Return & Tax Payment

What it is: Monthly summary return where the business declares total output tax, claims ITC and pays net GST. This involves actual cash payment — wrong figures mean a tax demand or excess outflow.

Why it's complex: Requires consolidating GSTR-1 output + GSTR-2B ITC + RCM liability + ITC reversal (Rule 42/43) + ITC utilisation sequence — IGST first, then CGST/SGST. Cross-utilisation of CGST with SGST is not permitted.

Manual process (2–3 hrs):

1
Prepare outward tax summary from GSTR-1 working — CGST / SGST / IGST by supply type
2
Calculate eligible ITC from 2B reconciliation — less Sec 17(5), less Rule 42/43 reversal
3
Calculate RCM liability — unregistered vendor purchases attract reverse charge
4
Apply ITC utilisation order — IGST→IGST→CGST→SGST (no cross-utilisation)
5
Compute net cash payable per head — CGST / SGST / IGST / Cess
6
Enter on portal, pay, file

With AI (20 min review):

Read 2B reconciliation ITC outputAI
Identify & compute RCM liabilityAI
Apply Rule 42/43 ITC reversalAI
Apply prescribed utilisation sequenceAI
Show net cash per head with credit balanceAI
Review computation, portal entry & filingHUMAN

7. TDS — Tax Deducted at Source

What it is: Payer deducts tax at payment time and deposits via Challan 281 (by 7th of month). Quarterly return (24Q / 26Q / 27EQ) filed on TRACES. TDS certificates Form 16 / 16A issued after filing.

Key sections in daily practice:
192 — Salary  |  194C — Contractor (1%)  |  194J — Professional/Technical (10%)  |  194I — Rent (10%)
194A — Interest  |  194H — Commission  |  206AA — Missing PAN → 20% deduction

Manual process (8–10 hrs/quarter):

1
Collect salary register, vendor payments, rent, professional fees for the quarter
2
Map each payment to section; check thresholds (194C: ₹30k single/₹1L cumulative; 194I: ₹2.4L/year)
3
Validate PAN — invalid or missing = 20% deduction under Sec 206AA
4
Check 15G/15H certificates and Sec 197 lower deduction orders on file
5
Calculate TDS per deductee; track cumulative payments; flag threshold breaches
6
Reconcile against Challan 281 payments; calculate interest u/s 201 for late deposit
7
Enter in TDS software, generate FVU file, upload to TRACES, file return
8
Generate and issue Form 16 / 16A to deductees
