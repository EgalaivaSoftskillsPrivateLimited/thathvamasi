2. GSTR-1 — What It Is & How AI Automates It

What Is GSTR-1?

GSTR-1 is the statement of outward supplies — every sale invoice a business issues in a month must be reported here. It must be filed monthly (or quarterly for small businesses) on the GST portal.

What a GST Team Does Today (Manual Process):

1
Collect sales data from the client Client sends: Sales register / Tally export / ERP export / Manual invoices as PDF or Excel.
2
Validate each invoice Check: Invoice number format, Date, GSTIN of buyer, Taxable value, Tax rate applied, HSN code, Place of supply (IGST vs CGST+SGST).
3
Classify supplies B2B (registered buyers) / B2C (unregistered, value above ₹2.5L) / B2CS (small unregistered) / Exports / Nil-rated / Exempt / RCM.
4
Prepare HSN summary Group invoices by HSN code, calculate total quantity and taxable value for each code.
5
Check for errors Duplicate invoice numbers, invalid GSTINs, wrong tax rates, missing details, value mismatches.
6
Prepare JSON / portal upload Convert working to GST portal format. Upload or enter data on the portal. File.

How the Intranet AI Automates This:

Collect data Accepts PDF invoices, Excel registers, Tally exports. Extracts all fields automatically. AI
Validate invoices Checks GSTIN format (15-digit), invoice numbering, tax rate vs HSN, place of supply logic. AI
Classify supplies Reads buyer GSTIN → checks if registered → classifies B2B / B2C / Export automatically. AI
HSN summary Groups by HSN code, computes totals per code, flags unusual HSN codes. AI
Error detection Flags duplicates, invalid GSTINs, rate mismatches, missing fields with line-by-line detail. AI

3. GSTR-2B — What It Is & How AI Automates It

What Is GSTR-2B?

GSTR-2B is an auto-drafted ITC (Input Tax Credit) statement generated by the GST portal. It shows all purchase invoices that your suppliers have uploaded in their GSTR-1. The client uses this to know which ITC they are eligible to claim.

The Core Problem Today:
The GSTR-2B from the portal must be matched against the company's own purchase register (books). Mismatches mean ITC is either over-claimed (risky) or under-claimed (money left on table). This matching is done manually, entry by entry, often involving thousands of records.

What a GST Team Does Today (Manual Process):

1
Download GSTR-2B from portal Available as JSON or Excel on the 14th of every month.
2
Get purchase register from client Client sends Tally export / ERP data / manual purchase Excel.
3
Match invoice by invoice For every entry in GSTR-2B: find the matching entry in purchase register using Supplier GSTIN + Invoice Number + Invoice Date + Amount.
4
Identify mismatches • Invoice in 2B but not in books (supplier filed, client hasn't recorded)
• Invoice in books but not in 2B (supplier hasn't filed yet)
• Amount difference between 2B and books
• GSTIN mismatch
• Date difference
5
Decide on ITC Only invoices in GSTR-2B can be claimed as ITC. Mismatches need resolution.
6
Prepare ITC working Eligible ITC, ineligible ITC (blocked credits u/s 17(5)), ITC to be reversed.

How the Intranet AI Automates This:

Step What AI Does Who Does It
Load GSTR-2B Reads JSON or Excel from portal. Parses all supplier entries, invoice-wise. AI
Load Purchase Register Reads Tally export, ERP data or manual Excel. Normalises formats. AI
Match invoices Fuzzy-matches on GSTIN + Invoice No + Amount (allows for small formatting differences). Assigns match status to every line. AI
Classify mismatches Categorises: Matched / In 2B not in Books / In Books not in 2B / Amount diff / GSTIN diff / Date diff. AI
Apply ITC rules Checks HSN codes against Section 17(5) blocked credit list. Flags ineligible ITC automatically. AI
Review exceptions Employee sees only the mismatch list with suggested action for each item. HUMAN
Prepare ITC working Generates ITC eligibility statement ready for GSTR-3B entry. AI

4. GSTR-3B — What It Is & How AI Automates It

What Is GSTR-3B?

GSTR-3B is the monthly summary return and tax payment form. The business declares its total outward supply tax, claims ITC, and pays the net GST liability. It must be filed every month, and the tax must be paid before filing. This is the most critical return because it involves actual tax payment.

Why This Is Difficult:
GSTR-3B requires consolidating data from GSTR-1 working (outward), GSTR-2B reconciliation (inward/ITC), and applying various rules for ITC reversal, RCM liability, and previous month adjustments. Getting any figure wrong means tax demand or excess payment.

What a GST Team Does Today (Manual Process):

1
Prepare outward tax summary From GSTR-1 working: total CGST, SGST, IGST on outward supplies. Separate taxable / nil-rated / exempt / zero-rated.
2
Calculate eligible ITC From GSTR-2B reconciliation: total eligible ITC, split by CGST / SGST / IGST / Cess. Less blocked credits, less ITC reversal (Rule 42/43 for personal use, exempt supplies).
3
Calculate RCM liability RCM (Reverse Charge Mechanism) applies to certain purchases from unregistered vendors or specified services. This becomes both a liability AND eligible ITC.
4
Carry forward / utilisation of ITC ITC utilisation order is prescribed: IGST first (against IGST, then CGST, then SGST). CGST only against CGST and IGST. SGST only against SGST and IGST. Cannot cross-utilise CGST with SGST.
5
Calculate net tax payable Output tax minus eligible ITC. Pay via Electronic Cash Ledger. Check for excess in Electronic Credit Ledger.
6
File GSTR-3B Enter all figures in portal. Make payment. File.

How the Intranet AI Automates This:

Step What AI Does Who Does It
Outward tax summary Reads directly from GSTR-1 working already processed. Splits by supply type and tax head automatically. AI
ITC calculation Reads from GSTR-2B reconciliation output. Applies Section 17(5) exclusions. Computes Rule 42/43 reversal if applicable. AI
RCM computation Identifies RCM transactions from purchase data. Calculates liability and ITC offset. AI
ITC utilisation order Applies prescribed utilisation sequence (IGST → CGST → SGST). Computes optimum utilisation automatically. AI
Net tax computation Calculates net payable per head (CGST/SGST/IGST/Cess). Checks credit ledger balance. Shows cash to be paid. AI
Professional review Employee reviews full computation. Checks for any adjustments. Approves figures. HUMAN
Portal entry & filing Employee enters figures in portal, makes payment, files. HUMAN

5. TDS — What It Is & How AI Automates It

What Is TDS?

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) is a system where the payer deducts tax at the time of payment and deposits it with the government. For example, if a company pays ₹1,00,000 to a contractor, it must deduct TDS at 1% (₹1,000) and pay only ₹99,000 to the contractor. The ₹1,000 goes to the government under the deductor's TAN.

Key TDS Compliance Activities:
• Quarterly TDS returns: Form 24Q (salary), 26Q (non-salary), 27Q (non-resident), 27EQ (TCS)
• Monthly TDS payment (Challan 281) by 7th of next month
• Annual TDS certificates: Form 16 (salary), Form 16A (non-salary)
• Reconciliation of TDS with Form 26AS / AIS of deductee

What a TDS Team Does Today (Manual Process):

1
Collect payment data from client Client sends: Salary register / Vendor payment list / Contractor payments / Rent payments / Professional fee payments — for the quarter.
2
Identify TDS-applicable transactions Check each payment against Section 192 (salary), 194C (contractor), 194J (professional), 194I (rent), 194A (interest), 194H (commission) etc. Check threshold limits.
3
Verify PAN of deductees If PAN is missing or invalid: TDS rate doubles (Section 206AA). Must identify all such cases.
4
Check TDS certificates / lower deduction Some deductees provide Form 15G / 15H (nil deduction). Some provide lower deduction certificate u/s 197. Must be verified and applied.
5
Calculate TDS amount Apply correct rate per section. Check cumulative payments (threshold may be crossed during year). Calculate on gross or net depending on section.
6
Reconcile with challan payments TDS deposited via Challan 281. Must match challan amounts with computed TDS. Check for short deduction, late payment, interest liability.
7
Prepare quarterly TDS return Enter all deductee details in TDS software (TRACES / Winman / ClearTDS). Generate FVU file. Upload to TRACES portal.
8
Issue TDS certificates After return filing, generate Form 16 / 16A from TRACES. Send to deductees within due date.

How the Intranet AI Automates This:

Step What AI Does Who Does It
Collect & parse data Reads salary registers, vendor payment sheets. Extracts: payee name, PAN, payment type, amount, date. AI

8. Intranet AI Setup — What Is Needed
Hardware (One-Time):
• 1 dedicated server / workstation
• Minimum: 32GB RAM, 8-core CPU
• Better: NVIDIA GPU (faster processing)
• 2TB SSD storage
• Connected to office LAN
Software:
• Linux Ubuntu Server (free)
• Local AI model (Llama 3 / Mistral — free)
• FastAPI backend (already built)
• PostgreSQL database (already built)
• Browser-based dashboard (React)
Key Point: Once the server is set up, there is no per-query cost. Unlike ChatGPT which charges per use, the intranet AI runs as many queries as needed at zero marginal cost. The investment is the server hardware (₹1.5–3 lakh) and development cost.
