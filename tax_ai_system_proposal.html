Private AI System for Income Tax, GST & Accounting Work

An AI Assistant That Actually Does the Work — Not Just Answers Questions

For Tax Practitioners, Accountants, Finance Teams & Compliance Professionals

1. What Are We Building?

We are proposing a private AI system for Income Tax, GST, Accounting and Compliance work.

The system is designed to help finance and tax professionals with the actual day-to-day work they perform.

It can:
  • Read client documents
  • Extract financial information
  • Check data and compare different records
  • Find mistakes and mismatches
  • Prepare GST working and Income Tax working
  • Prepare reconciliation reports
  • Calculate and summarize figures
  • Prepare filing-related data
  • Generate Excel sheets and professional PDF reports
  • Prepare client communication
  • Track missing documents and pending work
  • Prepare notices and responses
  • Prepare proposals
  • Help employees complete cases faster
    • In simple words:
    Instead of an employee doing every step manually, the AI does the repetitive preparation and checking work, and the professional reviews and approves it.

    3. Example: GST Filing Workflow

    Step 1 — Read the Documents

    The system reads invoices and reports, extracting relevant information:

    - GSTIN
    - Invoice number & date
    - Taxable value
    - CGST, SGST, IGST
    - Supplier/customer details

    Step 2 — Compare Records

    Identifies:

    Purchase Register vs. GSTR-2B
    • Matching invoices
    • Missing invoices
    • Duplicate invoices
    • Amount differences
    • GSTIN differences

    Step 3 — Prepare GST Working

    Prepares working for:

    - GSTR-1
    - GSTR-3B
    - ITC Reconciliation
    - Outward supplies
    - Tax liability

    Step 4 — Generate Reports

    Creates professional outputs:

    GST REVIEW REPORT

    Client: ABC Private Limited
    Period: September 2026

    Particular Amount
    Sales ₹25,40,000
    Taxable Sales ₹21,50,000
    Output GST ₹3,87,000
    Eligible ITC ₹2,45,000
    Net GST Payable ₹1,42,000

    Attention Required: 18 invoices need verification, 7 invoices have tax amount differences, 3 duplicate invoices detected

    Status: Pending Professional Review

    Step 5 — Professional Review

    AI prepares → Employee reviews → If correct: Approve → Proceed with filing
    The AI does not replace the professional's responsibility.
