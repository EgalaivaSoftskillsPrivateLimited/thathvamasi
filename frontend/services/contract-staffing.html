Frequently Asked Questions

Contract Staffing FAQs

Essential details on employer liabilities, statutory filings, and billing arrangements.

Who is legally considered the employer of the contract staff?

Thathvamasi HR Consultancy is the official Employer of Record (EOR). All appointment letters are issued on our rolls, and we assume full legal responsibility for wage payments, EPF, ESI, gratuity, and labor law compliance under the Contract Labor (Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970.

How does client billing and statutory proof submission work?

At the end of each monthly attendance cycle, we generate a consolidated invoice covering gross wages, employer statutory contributions (EPF, ESI), and our management markup fee. We attach official bank-stamped EPF TRRN challans and ESI e-challans to ensure complete audit compliance for your finance team.

Can we absorb a contract employee into our permanent payroll?

Yes. We offer transparent temp-to-perm conversion clauses. After an associate completes a predefined duration on THC rolls (typically 6 months), you can transition them to your direct payroll with zero friction or dispute.

What is your turnaround time for replacing an underperforming contract associate?

If an associate fails to meet your performance standards, we initiate an immediate replacement search and deploy an alternate qualified candidate within 48 to 72 business hours.

How do you ensure compliance with minimum wage notifications?

Our legal compliance desk monitors state government gazette notifications for revisions to basic wages and Variable Dearness Allowance (VDA) across Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and other operating states, ensuring your wage structures are adjusted immediately without triggering non-compliance notices.
