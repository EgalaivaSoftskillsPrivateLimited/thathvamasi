Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide ERP & CRM software development services in Coimbatore & Kerala?

Yes, we deliver customized ERP & CRM software development across Coimbatore, Kochi, Bangalore, and Pan-India. We develop ERP and CRM platforms to manage sales, customers, finance, inventory, employees, workflows, and business processes with local engineering support.

How do I know if my business needs an ERP system?

If you're managing multiple departments using spreadsheets or disconnected software, an ERP system will streamline operations, eliminate data silos, improve financial visibility, and increase team efficiency immediately.

Can the software be customized for our specific business workflows?

Yes. Every enterprise operates differently. We design and build tailor-made data schemas, approval hierarchies, reporting dashboards, and role permissions that fit your exact commercial process.

What modules can be included in an ERP solution?

ERP systems can include inventory tracking, financial double-entry accounting, automated billing, HR & payroll with 2026 Labor Code compliance, vendor procurement, production planning, and real-time executive analytics.

Can the ERP system integrate with existing software and WhatsApp?

Absolutely. We connect ERP and CRM solutions with existing accounting software (Tally, Zoho), payment gateways (Razorpay, Cashfree), biometric attendance devices, and automated WhatsApp Business workflows.

Do you provide ongoing support, maintenance, and training?

Yes. We provide complete onboarding, employee role training, SLA-backed maintenance, automated database backups, security patches, and scalable feature upgrades as your operations expand.
