Frequently Asked Questions

Permanent Staffing FAQs

Clear answers on our contingency terms, 48-hour delivery SLAs, and replacement guarantees.

What is your delivery SLA for presenting lateral candidate shortlists?

We deliver an authenticated shortlist of 3 to 5 pre-screened candidates within 48 business hours of receiving your requisition. For niche or super-specialized skills, delivery is completed within 3 to 5 business days.

What are your commercial terms for permanent recruitment?

We work purely on a contingency basis with zero advance payment. You are invoiced only upon the candidate’s physical Day 1 joining at your workplace. Commercial fees are calculated as a percentage of the annual fixed CTC.

What replacement guarantee do you provide if a candidate leaves?

We offer an ironclad 90-day free replacement warranty. If a placed candidate leaves your company or is separated within 90 calendar days from joining, we will provide a replacement candidate at no extra cost.

How do you combat counter-offers and candidate dropouts during notice periods?

We verify resignation submission within 48 hours of offer acceptance. Furthermore, our dedicated talent engagement team conducts structured weekly check-ins with candidates throughout their notice period, achieving a 94.2% joining conversion rate.

Do you verify candidate background and salary credentials before sending?

Yes. Every dossier sent to your team includes verified EPFO service history, educational credential checks, and validated salary pay slips showing exact fixed vs variable components.
