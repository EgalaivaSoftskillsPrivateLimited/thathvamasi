Practice FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our engagement models across both talent recruitment and statutory corporate advisory.

How does Thathvamasi differ from conventional recruitment agencies?

Unlike resume brokers who simply forward unfiltered portal CVs, Thathvamasi operates dedicated industrial and deep-tech pods headed by former engineers and CAs. Every candidate presented is audited against official EPFO tenure records, past salary slips, and technical evaluations, backed by our 48-hour shortlist SLA and 90-day free replacement warranty.

Can Thathvamasi handle both our leadership hiring and our corporate MCA / GST filings?

Yes. Thathvamasi operates as a dual-division corporate partner. Our Division I manages CXO search, plant engineering staffing, and turnkey RPO pods, while Division II provides corporate secretarial services, Pvt Ltd incorporation within 3 business days, monthly GST returns, annual MCA filings (AOC-4 / MGT-7), trademark protection, and virtual CFO oversight.

What are your typical commercial models across services?

We offer flexible commercial arrangements: 100% contingency (no advance, pay only on candidate Day 1 physical joining) for permanent lateral staffing; milestone retained search for CXO roles; and flat transparent government filing fees plus nominal professional fees for company incorporation, GST, and trademark mandates.

What geographic corridors do you cover across India?

Headquartered in Coimbatore, we have active sourcing footprints, corporate clients, and legal delivery capabilities across Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kochi, Pune, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and international reach for remote engineering GCC setups.
