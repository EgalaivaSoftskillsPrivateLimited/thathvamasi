Frequently Asked Questions

HR Advisory & Compliance FAQs

Key insights on POSH Internal Committee requirements, labor code penalties, and audit defense.

Is appointing an external member to the POSH Internal Committee mandatory?

Yes. Under Section 4 of the POSH Act, 2013, every enterprise employing 10 or more employees must constitute an Internal Committee (IC) with at least one external member from an NGO or an individual familiar with sexual harassment issues. Failure to do so incurs strict statutory financial penalties and can lead to cancellation of business operating licenses.

What are the major liabilities facing companies under the 2026 Labor Codes?

The four New Labor Codes consolidate 29 central labor laws, introduce strict wage definitions where basic wages must form at least 50% of total CTC, mandate gig and contract worker social security, and escalate non-compliance liabilities significantly with stringent prosecution for repeated defaults. We assist enterprises in restructuring compensation models proactively.

How does Thathvamasi assist when a factory or labor inspector issues a notice?

Our legal compliance desk reviews the notice, examines the alleged register or contribution deficiency, drafts a formal legal reply backed by relevant case law, and coordinates directly with the inspecting officer to resolve the matter and avoid punitive prosecution.

Do you draft bilingual employment handbooks and standing orders?

Yes. For manufacturing and industrial facilities in South India, we draft Standing Orders and Employee Code of Conduct documents in both English and vernacular languages (Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada, Hindi) to fulfill statutory certification criteria.

What are the annual POSH reporting obligations for companies in Tamil Nadu?

Every organization must prepare an Annual POSH Report by January 31st each calendar year detailing the number of complaints received, disposed of, and employee training sessions conducted, and submit it to the designated District Officer (Social Welfare Department) as well as include it in their annual Board Directors' Report.
