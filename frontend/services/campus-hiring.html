Frequently Asked Questions

Campus Recruitment FAQs

Common questions regarding college drive timing, student dropouts, and assessment workflows.

When is the ideal time to schedule campus recruitment drives in Tamil Nadu?

The primary placement season across engineering and management colleges in Tamil Nadu runs from August to November for final-year students (Day 1 and Day 2 slots). A secondary placement window operates from January to March. We recommend booking dates at least 4 to 6 weeks in advance to secure prime calendar priority.

How do you mitigate student dropouts between campus offer and graduation?

Dropouts typically happen when students feel neglected during the 4 to 6 month wait before graduation. We operate an active post-offer engagement program including virtual town halls with company leaders, pre-onboarding technical challenges, branded welcome merchandise, and monthly check-ins, achieving a 92%+ joining conversion rate.

Can you execute completely virtual campus drives?

Yes. We execute end-to-end virtual campus drives utilizing cloud-based assessment software with automated webcam proctoring, synchronized online presentation webinars, and breakout rooms for virtual technical and HR interview panels.

What is the typical commercial model for campus recruitment?

We offer flexible pricing: either a turnkey project fee covering complete university coordination, online testing, and on-site logistics; or a hybrid model combining a modest drive execution retainer with a per-joined trainee success fee.
