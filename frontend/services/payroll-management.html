    Frequently Asked Questions

    Payroll Management FAQs

    Clear answers regarding our processing guarantees, data security, and statutory timelines.

    How does the 2026 Code on Wages impact our current salary structures?

    The Code on Wages mandates that Basic Pay plus Dearness Allowance must constitute at least 50% of the employee’s gross remuneration. Allowances exceeding 50% will be deemed as wages for calculating EPF, gratuity, and retiral liabilities. We restructure compensation packages to minimize the financial impact on your bottom line.

    How is employee salary and tax data kept confidential and secure?

    All client payroll computations are conducted on ISO 27001-aligned secure servers with role-based access control. All communication, bank files, and pay slips are encrypted with 256-bit AES encryption.

    What is your turnaround time for Full & Final (F&F) settlements?

    We deliver audited Full & Final statements within 7 business days from receipt of exit clearance and asset handover verification. Statements include gratuity, leave encashment, and statutory deduction reconciliations.

    Do you assist our employees with their annual income tax declarations?

    Yes. We manage both beginning-of-year tax declarations and year-end actual investment proof verification (under 80C, 80D, HRA rent receipts, home loan interest) across both the Old and New Tax Regimes.

    What happens if a statutory penalty is incurred due to a payroll delay?

    Under our contractual Service Level Agreement, we guarantee 100% on-time filing. In the rare event of a delay attributable to our team, Thathvamasi indemnifies and absorbs any statutory penalty fees.
