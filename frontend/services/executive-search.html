    Frequently Asked Questions

    Executive Search FAQs

    Everything you need to know about retained search, confidentiality, and our placement guarantees.

    How does retained executive search differ from contingency staffing?

    Contingency staffing is volume-driven and relies on active job seekers. Retained executive search is an exclusive, consultative partnership where our Senior Partners commit dedicated resources to discreetly target and engage the top 1% of passive leaders who are not on the job market.

    How do you safeguard client and candidate confidentiality?

    All outreach is conducted under strict NDAs using blind profiles. The hiring company’s identity is only revealed to vetted, interested candidates after thorough mutual alignment and formal sponsor approval.

    What is the typical timeframe to present a calibrated CXO shortlist?

    A fully vetted, interviewed, and scored shortlist of 3 to 5 prime executives is typically presented within 18 to 24 business days from kickoff. Board interviews and offer negotiations typically complete in 4 to 8 weeks.

    What replacement warranty do you offer on executive appointments?

    We provide an industry-leading 90-day replacement warranty. If an executive departs or is terminated for cause within 90 days of joining, we initiate an immediate, priority replacement search at zero additional professional fees.

    Do you assist with executive compensation benchmarking and buyout structuring?

    Yes. We offer comprehensive CTC structuring guidance covering fixed salary, performance-linked variable pay (STI), long-term equity / ESOP grants, retention bonuses, and non-compete notice buyout negotiations.
