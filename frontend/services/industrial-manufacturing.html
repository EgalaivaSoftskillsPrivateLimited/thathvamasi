Frequently Asked Questions

Industrial Staffing FAQs

Answers regarding our engineering sourcing methods, notice period handling, and replacement terms.

Why is plant engineering talent difficult to hire through standard job portals?

High-performing manufacturing engineers, foundry managers, and CNC programmers spend their working days on the shop floor, not monitoring LinkedIn or job boards. Sourcing them requires specialized recruiters with personal relationships in local engineering clusters who can engage them discreetly through peer networks.

What geographic industrial clusters does Thathvamasi cover?

While headquartered in Coimbatore, we actively source candidates across Tamil Nadu (Chennai, Sriperumbudur, Oragadam, Hosur, Tirupur, Salem), Karnataka (Bangalore, Peenya, Belgaum), Maharashtra (Pune, Chakan, Aurangabad), and Gujarat (Ahmedabad, Rajkot).

How do you verify a manufacturing candidate’s technical claims?

Our technical interviewers drill down on specific equipment models (e.g. Mazak, DMG Mori, DISA), casting yield calculations, cycle time reductions, and scrap percentages achieved. We verify authentic EPFO service records and conduct discreet reference checks with past plant managing directors.

What replacement warranty do you offer on permanent manufacturing hires?

We provide a contractual 90-day free replacement guarantee. If a placed professional separates from your company within 90 days of joining, we provide a replacement candidate at zero additional professional fees.
