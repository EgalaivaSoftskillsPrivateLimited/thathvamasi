Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Thathvamasi Business Solutions Private Limited

1. Introduction & Commitment to Confidentiality

Thathvamasi Business Solutions Private Limited (referred to as "Thathvamasi", "we", "us", or "our") operates as a dual-division enterprise providing corporate advisory, company incorporation, taxation, MCA statutory compliance, Virtual CFO services, executive search, and human resource consultancy.

We are committed to preserving the privacy, confidentiality, and integrity of corporate records, personal identity information, and financial documents entrusted to us by clients, directors, shareholders, partners, and job candidates in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), the Information Technology Act 2000, and related statutory guidelines in India.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver statutory filings and executive recruitment services, we collect necessary data categories including:

  • Corporate & Entity Information: Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association (e-MOA, e-AOA), LLP agreements, partnership deeds, PAN, TAN, GSTIN, Udyam MSME certificates, and audited balance sheets.
  • Director & Shareholder KYC: Permanent Account Number (PAN), Aadhaar, passport copies, voter ID, Director Identification Numbers (DIN/DPIN), Class-3 Digital Signature Certificates (DSC), and residential address proofs required for MCA SPICe+ and RoC filings.
  • Financial & Tax Data: Inward/outward invoice registers, GSTR-2B ITC reports, TDS payment challans, bank CMA project data, wage slips, and accounting ledgers.
  • Candidate Profiles (HR Division): Curriculum vitae (CVs), educational credentials, employment history, compensation details, references, and professional certifications.

3. Purpose of Processing Your Data

Your information is processed strictly for the following professional and statutory objectives:

  • Preparing and submitting statutory electronic filings before the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), Registrar of Companies (ROC), Goods & Services Tax Network (GSTN), Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), EPFO, and ESIC.
  • Structuring capital debt dossiers, CMA project reports, and financial projections for commercial bank loan sanctions.
  • Conducting executive talent matching, candidate pre-screening, interview scheduling, and placement facilitation with hiring client organizations under mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs).
  • Issuing statutory notices, filing receipts, and compliance audit reports.

4. Disclosure & Data Security

We do not sell, rent, or commercialize your personal or corporate data to third parties. Disclosure is strictly confined to:

  • Authorised statutory portals (MCA21, GST portal, Income Tax portal, EPFO/ESIC portals) upon client authorization.
  • Nationalised or private scheduled commercial banks evaluating loan dossiers requested by the client.
  • Prospective employer organizations reviewing verified candidate dossiers under strict confidentiality.

All electronic documents are stored on access-controlled, encrypted corporate cloud infrastructure with multi-factor authentication (MFA) and restricted employee privilege tiers.

5. Data Retention & Rights

Statutory records and filings are retained in accordance with the mandatory record-keeping periods specified under the Companies Act 2013 and Goods and Services Tax Act (minimum 8 financial years). Candidates may request updating, correction, or withdrawal of their resumes from our active placement database at any time by contacting our desk.
