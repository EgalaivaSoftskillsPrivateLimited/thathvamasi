Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our executive recruitment SLAs and statutory business advisory.

What is your 48-Hour Shortlist SLA guarantee?

Upon finalizing a role requisition, our practice leads tap our proprietary network of 18,500+ pre-calibrated leaders to deliver 3 to 5 fully vetted, interview-ready candidate profiles within 48 business hours.

How does the 90-day placement replacement warranty work?

If any placed candidate leaves or fails probationary performance criteria within 90 calendar days of joining, Thathvamasi initiates an expedited priority search to deliver a qualified replacement at zero additional fee.

How quickly can you incorporate a Private Limited Company?

Our corporate advisory team completes SPICe+ MCA name reservation, DSC generation, DIN allotment, and Certificate of Incorporation (CoI) issuance within 3 business days, bundled with PAN, TAN, and corporate bank account opening.

Do you handle monthly GST and annual RoC filings for startups and SMEs?

Yes. Our retainers cover monthly GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B filings, ITC reconciliation, annual GSTR-9, RoC Forms AOC-4 & MGT-7, and Director DIR-3 KYC with zero penalty guarantees.

How does your Trademark (TM) application process work?

We conduct an exhaustive class search across all 45 statutory classes, draft and file your application with the Indian Trademark Registry to secure your official TM application receipt within 24 hours, and handle objection examination replies through to registration.
