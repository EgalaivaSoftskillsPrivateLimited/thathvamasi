Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries regarding bank loan preparation and project reports.

Can you guarantee a business loan?

No. Loan approval, sanctioned interest rates, collateral security requirements and fund disbursement are strictly determined by the lending institution based on its independent credit appraisal and banking norms.

Do you prepare project reports?

Yes. Detailed Project Report (DPR) preparation, techno-economic writeups, financial model spreadsheets and CMA data can be included based on your project requirements and agreed professional scope.

What documents may a bank request?

Requirements vary by lender and facility. Common documents include promoter KYC (PAN, Aadhaar), 3 years audited financials with Tax Audit reports, 12 months bank statements, 12 months GST returns, projected balance sheets, CMA data, quotation for proposed machinery and property ownership documents.

Can you help an existing business with working capital planning?

Yes. We review current debtors, creditors and inventory cycle times, recalculate drawing power, and prepare cash-flow forecasts to support discussions for enhancing Cash Credit (CC) or Overdraft (OD) limits.
