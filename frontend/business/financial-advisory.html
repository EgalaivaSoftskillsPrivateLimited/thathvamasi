From working capital renewals to greenfield project funding reports.

CMA Data Preparation (Forms I to VI)

Preparation of standardized Credit Monitoring Arrangement financial packages required by all scheduled commercial banks.

  • Form I: Existing & proposed working capital credit limits
  • Form II: Operating statement (revenue, direct costs & EBITDA)
  • Form III: Balance sheet analysis (tangible net worth & liabilities)
  • Form IV & V: Maximum Permissible Bank Finance (MPBF) calculation
  • Form VI: Fund-flow & cash-flow statement projections

Detailed Project Reports (DPR)

Exhaustive technical and financial feasibility dossiers for term loans, factory expansion, and machinery acquisition.

  • Executive business profile, industry outlook & promoter credentials
  • Technical project description, plant layout & machinery quotes
  • Total cost of project & means of finance (debt-equity mix)
  • 5 to 7 year projected P&L, balance sheet, and DSCR metrics
  • Break-even point (BEP) and sensitivity risk analysis

Financing FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding CMA data, DSCR ratios and collateral-free loan limits.

What is CMA Data and why do banks require it?

CMA (Credit Monitoring Arrangement) data is a standardized financial report required by banks to analyze past performance and 5-year projections, ensuring that the borrower has sufficient operational cash flow to service debt.

What is an acceptable DSCR ratio for a bank term loan?

Most commercial banks look for an average Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) between 1.50 and 2.00, demonstrating that the company generates 1.5 to 2 times the cash required to pay annual interest and principal installments.

What is the CGTMSE loan scheme?

Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) provides collateral-free loans up to ₹5 Crores for eligible manufacturing and service enterprises without requiring real estate collateral.

Can you help enhance existing bank CC/OD limits?

Yes. If your business turnover has expanded and current limits are choked, we analyze your projected revenue and inventory cycles to structure an enhancement proposal for your bank credit committee.
