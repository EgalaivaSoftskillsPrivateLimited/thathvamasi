Our Bank Loan Support Services

Professional financial documentation prepared to satisfy stringent banking credit appraisal norms.

Business Loan Documentation

Assembling a coherent, audit-backed loan application file for nationalised and private commercial lenders.

  • Initial review of funding requirement & debt-serviceability
  • Business profile and historical financial compilation
  • Comprehensive documentation checklist & KYC dossiers
  • Supporting banking query responses within agreed scope

Detailed Project Report (DPR) Preparation

Comprehensive techno-economic feasibility reports presenting investment viability to credit managers.

  • Business background, promoters' pedigree & strategic objectives
  • Total project cost, promoter equity contribution & debt requirement
  • Projected profit and loss, balance sheets & cash flows
  • Working capital estimates, DSCR ratios and break-even analysis

CMA Data & Working Capital Advisory

Credit Monitoring Arrangement (CMA) data preparation for Cash Credit (CC) and Overdraft (OD) sanction.

  • Past 2 years audited, current year estimated & 3 years projected CMA sheets
  • Review of debtor holding periods, inventory turnover & creditors
  • Maximum Permissible Bank Finance (MPBF) method calculations
  • Liquidity review and working capital restructuring support

Financial Readiness Review

Before approaching a commercial bank or financial institution, we help organise your existing financial records and identify gaps that may require attention. This diagnostic review covers accounting ledger consistency, past tax returns, bank statement banking transactions, projected debt-service coverage ratio (DSCR), and supporting collateral documentation.

Financing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries regarding bank loan preparation and project reports.

Can you guarantee a business loan?

No. Loan approval, sanctioned interest rates, collateral security requirements and disbursement are strictly determined by the lending institution based on its independent credit appraisal and banking norms.

Do you prepare project reports?

Yes. Detailed Project Report (DPR) preparation, techno-economic writeups, financial model spreadsheets and CMA data are prepared based on your project requirements and agreed professional scope.

What documents may a bank request?

Common requests include promoter KYC (PAN, Aadhaar), 3 years audited financials with Tax Audit reports, 12 months bank statements, 12 months GST returns, projected balance sheets, CMA data, quotation for proposed machinery and property ownership documents.

Can you help an existing business with working capital planning?

Yes. We review current debtors, creditors and inventory cycle times, recalculate drawing power, and prepare cash-flow forecasts to support discussions for enhancing Cash Credit (CC) or Overdraft (OD) limits.
