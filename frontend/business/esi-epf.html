Labour FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding applicability thresholds, wage limits and late payment damages.

When does EPF registration become mandatory?

EPF registration is mandatory for any commercial establishment or factory employing 20 or more persons. Establishments with fewer than 20 employees can also register voluntarily.

What is the wage threshold for ESIC coverage?

ESIC applies to employees earning gross monthly wages up to ₹21,000 (or ₹25,000 for employees with disabilities). Once gross salary exceeds this threshold, the employee is exempt from ESIC.

What is the monthly filing due date for EPF and ESI?

Both EPF and ESI monthly contribution returns and payments must be completed on or before the 15th of the following month. Delays attract statutory interest under Section 7Q and damages under Section 14B.

Can a director or partner be covered under EPF?

Managing directors or working directors drawing a regular contractual salary can be covered under EPF, provided their employment relationship and wage structure meet EPFO rules.
