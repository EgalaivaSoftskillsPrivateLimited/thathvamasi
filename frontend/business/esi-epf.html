Payroll Compliance FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information on employee thresholds, ceilings and monthly filing dates.

Is EPF compulsory for every employer?

No. EPF registration is mandatory for establishments employing 20 or more persons. However, voluntary registration is permitted with the consent of the majority of employees even with fewer staff.

Is ESI applicable to every employee?

No. ESI coverage is applicable to employees earning gross monthly wages up to ₹21,000 (₹25,000 for employees with disabilities) in establishments employing 10 or more persons.

Can you help with monthly payroll-related compliance?

Yes. Recurring monthly assistance can be structured based on employee count, payroll complexity, UAN additions/exits and monthly ECR generation before the 15th of every month.

Can you help with pending contributions or filings?

We review historical wage records, compute accrued statutory interest under Section 7Q and damages under Section 14B, and coordinate a systematic regularisation plan with the department.
