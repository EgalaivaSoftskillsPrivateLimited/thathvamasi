Statutory FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to employer thresholds, deadlines and coverage rules.

Is EPF compulsory for every employer?

No. EPF registration is mandatory for establishments employing 20 or more persons. However, voluntary registration is permitted with the consent of the majority of employees even with fewer staff.

Is ESI applicable to every employee?

No. ESI coverage is applicable to employees earning gross monthly wages up to ₹21,000 (₹25,000 for persons with disabilities) in establishments employing 10 or more persons in notified areas.

Can you help with monthly payroll-related compliance?

Yes. Recurring monthly assistance can be structured based on employee headcount, payroll complexity, UAN additions/exits and monthly ECR generation before the 15th of every month.

Can you help with pending contributions or filings?

We can review available historical records, identify outstanding dues, compute interest under Section 7Q and damages under Section 14B, and coordinate a systematic compliance regularisation plan.
