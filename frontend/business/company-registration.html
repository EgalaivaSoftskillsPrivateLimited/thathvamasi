Execution Lifecycle

The 5-Phase Incorporation Roadmap

Our structured incorporation methodology ensures zero ROC query rejections, timely name reservations, and immediate operational readiness.

MCA SPICe+ READY • ZERO DEFECT
Phase 01 / Discovery Day 1

Name Reservation & Structure Advisory

We check name availability under MCA guidelines and trademark databases, select optimal authorized share capital, and file the RUN (Reserve Unique Name) application.

Phase 02 / Digital Identity Day 2

Class-3 DSC & Director KYC

We issue secure Class-3 Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) with video verification for all promoters and collect ID and registered office utility proofs.

Phase 03 / Filing Day 3

Drafting Charter & SPICe+ Electronic Submission

Drafting customized e-MOA and e-AOA statutory charters, assembling DIR-2 consent forms, INC-9 declarations, and uploading the comprehensive SPICe+ Part B dossier to MCA CRC.

Phase 04 / Certification Day 4

Certificate of Incorporation & Tax Identifiers

Receipt of the official Certificate of Incorporation (COI) featuring the Corporate Identification Number (CIN), Permanent Account Number (PAN), and Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN).

Phase 05 / Activation Day 5

Bank Current Account & INC-20A Commencement

Opening the company current account, depositing subscribed share capital, and filing Form INC-20A (Commencement of Business) with ROC to enable legal commercial operations.

Documents Generally Required

Keep these documents ready to ensure fast 3-day turnaround.

Directors & Partners KYC

  • PAN card of all directors/partners
  • Identity proof (Voter ID, Passport or Driving Licence)
  • Address proof (Bank statement or utility bill within 2 months)
  • Passport-size photographs & mobile/email verification

Registered Office Evidence

  • Electricity bill, gas bill or property tax receipt (latest)
  • Rent agreement / Lease deed on stamp paper
  • No-Objection Certificate (NOC) from property owner
  • Residential addresses are legally acceptable as office

Business Profile Details

  • Proposed 2-3 company names in priority order
  • Main business objects & operational activity description
  • Proposed authorized & paid-up equity share capital
  • Percentage of shareholding split among promoters
Incorporation FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding timelines, home office registration and post-incorporation steps.

How long does company registration take?

The timeline generally takes between 3 to 5 working days, depending on document readiness, MCA name approval speed, and government processing times at CRC.

Which is better: Private Limited or LLP?

A Private Limited Company is preferable for businesses seeking investor funding, equity shareholding and corporate brand presence. An LLP is ideal for service firms and partnerships looking for lower recurring compliance costs.

Can I register a company from my home address?

Yes. A residential home address or rented apartment can be registered as the company's official registered office, provided an electricity bill and owner NOC are submitted.

Is GST included in company registration?

GST registration is a separate statutory tax registration. We coordinate your GST registration immediately alongside or right after incorporation.
