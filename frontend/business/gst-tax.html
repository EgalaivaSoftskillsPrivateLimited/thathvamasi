Tax FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding filing dates, ITC conditions and tax audits.

When is the monthly GST filing deadline?

For regular taxpayers, GSTR-1 (outward supplies) is due on the 11th of every month, and GSTR-3B (tax computation & payment) is due on the 20th of every month.

Can I claim ITC if the supplier has not filed GSTR-1?

Under current GST law, Input Tax Credit can only be claimed if the corresponding invoice appears in your auto-populated GSTR-2B statement. We help identify missing suppliers proactively.

What is the threshold for a Tax Audit under Section 44AB?

For businesses with more than 95% digital transactions, the tax audit threshold is ₹10 Crores turnover. For businesses with cash transactions exceeding 5%, the threshold is ₹1 Crore.

Can you assist with GST Department notices?

Yes. We assist with drafting detailed factual replies to ASMT-10 scrutiny notices, DRC-01 demand intimations, and reconciliation mismatches before GST adjudicating authorities.
