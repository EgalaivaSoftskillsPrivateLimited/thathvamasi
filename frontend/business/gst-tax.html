Taxation FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions on GST applicability, deadlines, and previous-period regularisation.

Is GST registration compulsory for every business?

No. Applicability generally depends on aggregate annual turnover exceeding statutory thresholds (₹40 Lakhs for goods, ₹20 Lakhs for services in Tamil Nadu), interstate sales, or mandatory categories such as e-commerce.

Do you file both GST and income tax returns?

Yes. We offer coordinated filing support for both monthly GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) and annual corporate/individual income tax returns (ITR), ensuring complete reconciliation between both portals.

Can you help with previous-period returns?

Yes. We review outstanding unfiled periods, assess applicable late fees under Section 47 and interest under Section 50, and coordinate a systematic catch-up filing plan to restore active GSTIN status.

Can you guarantee a tax refund?

No. Tax refund eligibility and disbursement are governed by statutory provisions and jurisdictional tax authority scrutiny. We assist by preparing accurate claim applications with all supporting invoices.
