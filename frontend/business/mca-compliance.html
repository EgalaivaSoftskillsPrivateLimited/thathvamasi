Our Corporate Compliance Services

Comprehensive secretarial management for Private Limited companies, OPCs and LLPs under the Indian Companies Act.

Annual ROC Company Filings

Statutory annual disclosures ensuring your company maintains active corporate status with the Registrar of Companies.

  • Form AOC-4 balance sheet & P&L electronic filing
  • Form MGT-7 annual return (or MGT-7A for small entities)
  • Board’s Report review and statutory disclosure drafting
  • AGM notice, directors' attendance and minutes drafting support

Director & DIN Compliance

Annual KYC updates and board changes to prevent director deactivation and statutory disqualification.

  • Annual DIR-3 KYC Web verification & e-Form filing
  • Director appointment, resignation & removal (Form DIR-12)
  • Statutory auditor appointment filings (Form ADT-1)
  • DIN reactivation support for deactivated director numbers

LLP Annual & Event Filings

Mandatory disclosures under the Limited Liability Partnership Act to protect designated partners.

  • Form 11 Annual Return filing (due by May 30th)
  • Form 8 Statement of Account & Solvency (due October 30th)
  • Partner admission, resignation & Form 4 filings
  • LLP Agreement amendments and supplemental deed filings

Corporate Compliance Health Check

A structured diagnostic audit across historical MCA filings to identify hidden gaps or pending forms.

  • Review of entity master data and past 3 years' ROC history
  • Verification of charge registrations & satisfaction (CHG-1/4)
  • Statutory registers review (Members, Directors, Debentures)
  • Regularisation roadmap with estimated statutory fee schedule

Secretarial Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions on ROC deadlines, director disqualification, and late filings.

What is ROC compliance?

ROC compliance refers to mandatory annual and event-based filing obligations that companies and LLPs must submit to the Registrar of Companies under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).

Do companies and LLPs follow the same filing process?

No. Companies file Form AOC-4 (financials) and Form MGT-7 (annual return), while LLPs file Form 8 (statement of accounts) and Form 11 (annual return) under different statutory due dates.

Can you assist with overdue filings from earlier years?

Yes. We review outstanding historical periods, calculate the accrued statutory late fee schedule, and coordinate catch-up filings to restore your company to good standing.

Can you maintain an ongoing corporate compliance calendar?

Yes. As part of our annual retainer service, we maintain a customized compliance calendar with milestone reminders for AGMs, board meetings, director KYC and ROC filing dates.
