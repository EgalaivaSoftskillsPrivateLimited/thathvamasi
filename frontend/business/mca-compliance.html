Compliance FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding annual filing due dates, late fees and non-compliance consequences.

What is the penalty for late ROC filing?

Under the Companies Act, delayed filing of Form AOC-4 and MGT-7 attracts an additional statutory fee of ₹100 per day of delay per form, with no upper cap. Proactive filing prevents heavy cumulative fines.

What happens if a company does not file for consecutive years?

If a company fails to file annual returns for two consecutive years, the ROC may initiate strike-off proceedings and disqualify directors from holding office across any company for up to 5 years.

Is annual filing mandatory for small companies and OPCs?

Yes. Even if a company has zero revenue or commercial activity during the financial year, filing Form AOC-4 and Form MGT-7A (simplified for small companies and OPCs) is strictly mandatory.

What are the annual filing requirements for LLPs?

LLPs must file Form 11 (Annual Return) by May 30th and Form 8 (Statement of Account & Solvency) by October 30th every year. Delays also attract ₹100 per day statutory late fees.
