Audit FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding statutory requirements, coordination scope and audit schedules.

What is the difference between internal and statutory audit?

An internal audit is commissioned by management to evaluate internal controls and operational efficiency. A statutory audit is an independent review mandated by law to express an opinion on whether the financial statements present a true and fair view.

Who conducts the statutory audit?

Under the Companies Act, statutory audits must be conducted by independent, practicing Chartered Accountants. We assist with preparing all files and working papers to ensure a smooth, qualification-free audit.

What is CARO 2020?

The Companies (Auditor's Report) Order (CARO) 2020 requires statutory auditors to report on specific matters such as inventory records, loan defaults, internal audit adequacy, and timely payment of statutory dues.

Can you assist with due diligence for fundraising?

Yes. When an investor or bank consortium evaluates your business, we organize financial data rooms, reconcile historical tax filings, and prepare normalized EBITDA models for rigorous investor scrutiny.
