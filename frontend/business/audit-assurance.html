Audit FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key differences between internal audits and statutory engagements.

What is the difference between internal and statutory audit?

Internal audit generally evaluates business processes, records and internal controls for management governance. Statutory audit is required by law under the Companies Act and must be undertaken by an independent registered auditor.

Can every business conduct its own statutory audit?

No. Statutory audit requirements and auditor eligibility are strictly governed by legislation. Only qualified, independent Chartered Accountants holding a certificate of practice can conduct statutory audits.

Can you help prepare documents for an audit?

Yes. We organize accounting ledgers, reconcile balances, compile supporting vouchers and create comprehensive audit files to ensure a smooth audit process.

Will an audit guarantee that there are no errors or fraud?

No. An audit provides reasonable assurance according to its defined scope and professional sampling standards; it cannot guarantee the detection of every concealed error or instance of fraud.
