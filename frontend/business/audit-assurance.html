Assurance Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Key distinctions and scope clarifications regarding audit engagements.

What is the difference between internal and statutory audit?

Internal audit generally evaluates processes, operational efficiency, records and controls for internal management governance. Statutory audit is legally mandatory under the Companies Act for specified entities and must follow applicable professional auditing standards.

Can every business conduct its own statutory audit?

No. Statutory audit requirements and auditor eligibility are strictly governed by company law. Independent registered Chartered Accountants who meet independence criteria must conduct statutory audits.

Can you help prepare documents for an audit?

Yes. We specialize in organising accounting files, reconciling general ledgers, assembling supporting documentation, and preparing audit groupings to streamline the formal audit process.

Will an audit guarantee that there are no errors or fraud?

No. An audit provides reasonable assurance according to its defined scope and professional sampling standards; it does not guarantee detection of every error or concealed fraud.
