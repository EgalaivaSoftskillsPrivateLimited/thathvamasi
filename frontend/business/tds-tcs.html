Withholding Tax Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions on TDS rates, challan deposits and correction workflows.

What is TDS?

Tax Deducted at Source is a mechanism under which tax is deducted from specified payments (such as salaries, contractor fees, professional fees, or rent) according to applicable statutory tax rates.

What is TCS?

Tax Collected at Source applies to specified business sales transactions where the seller collects an additional tax percentage from the buyer under Section 206C of the Income Tax Act.

Are TDS returns filed monthly?

No. While tax deposits are made monthly (by the 7th of the following month), TDS return statements (Form 24Q, 26Q, 27EQ) are submitted quarterly (Q1 July 31, Q2 October 31, Q3 January 31, Q4 May 31).

Can you help correct an earlier return?

Yes. We review TRACES justification reports, identify PAN errors or uncredited challans, and coordinate online or offline correction statements (C1 to C9) to clear default notices.
