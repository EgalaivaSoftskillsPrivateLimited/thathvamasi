TDS FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding deposit dates, late fees and non-PAN consequences.

When is monthly TDS required to be deposited?

TDS deducted during any month must be deposited to the government treasury via Challan ITNS 281 on or before the 7th of the following month (except for March deductions, which are due by April 30th).

What is the penalty for late filing of TDS returns?

Under Section 234E of the Income Tax Act, a mandatory late filing fee of ₹200 per day is levied for every day of delay, up to the total amount of tax deductible in the return.

What happens if a vendor does not provide a PAN?

Under Section 206AA, if a deductee fails to furnish their valid PAN, TDS must be deducted at the higher of the statutory rate, rate in force, or a flat 20%.

What is Section 194Q vs Section 206C(1H)?

Section 194Q requires buyers with turnover exceeding ₹10 Cr to deduct 0.1% TDS on goods purchases exceeding ₹50 Lakhs. Section 206C(1H) is the corresponding TCS collected by sellers if the buyer has not deducted TDS.
