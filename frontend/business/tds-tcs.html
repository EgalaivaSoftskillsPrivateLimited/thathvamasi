Withholding Tax Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidelines on TDS rates, challan deposits and correction workflows.

What is TDS?

Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) is a mechanism under which tax is deducted by the payer from specified payments (such as salaries, contractor fees, professional fees, or rent) and deposited with the Central Government.

What is TCS?

Tax Collected at Source (TCS) applies to sellers who collect an additional tax percentage from buyers upon specified transactions (such as sale of motor vehicles above 10 Lakhs, scrap, or goods exceeding 50 Lakhs turnover under Section 206C(1H)).

Are TDS returns filed monthly?

No. While TDS payment/deposit is generally monthly (by the 7th of the subsequent month), TDS return statements (Form 24Q, 26Q, 27EQ) are filed quarterly (Q1 July 31, Q2 October 31, Q3 January 31, Q4 May 31).

Can you help correct an earlier return?

Yes. We review TRACES justification reports, identify PAN errors or challan mismatches, and file online/offline correction statements (C1 to C9) to clear defaults and demand notices.
