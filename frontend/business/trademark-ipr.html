Phase 01 / Clearance Search Day 1

Availability & Conflict Analysis

Phase 02 / Filing Day 2

Form TM-A Submission & Symbol Authorization

Drafting goods/services descriptions, preparing user affidavit, and submitting Form TM-A electronically to immediately secure your official application number and ™ symbol rights.

Phase 03 / Examination Months 1 – 3

IPR FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding symbol usage, government fees and protection validity.

When can I start using the TM symbol?

You can start using the ™ symbol immediately upon filing Form TM-A and obtaining your official application number. The registered ® symbol can only be used once the registration certificate is formally issued.

How long is a trademark valid in India?

A registered trademark is valid for 10 years from the date of application. It can be renewed indefinitely every 10 years by paying the prescribed renewal fee.

Is there a government fee discount for Startups & MSMEs?

Yes. Individuals, DPIIT Startups, and MSMEs with valid Udyam registration pay ₹4,500 government filing fee per class, compared to ₹9,000 for standard corporate applicants.

Do I need a Trade License in Coimbatore?

Yes. Any commercial establishment, office, shop, or manufacturing premises operating within the Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation limits is required to hold an active Trade Licence.
