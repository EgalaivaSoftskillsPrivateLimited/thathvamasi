Accounting FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to questions regarding bookkeeping, virtual CFO scope, and audits.

Is virtual CFO service the same as bookkeeping?

No. Bookkeeping focuses on accurately recording financial transactions. Virtual CFO support focuses on interpreting financial information, financial modeling, budgeting, cash-flow forecasting and executive strategic decisions.

Can I receive monthly financial reports?

Yes. Monthly management reports (MIS, P&L, balance sheet, debtor aging) are included in our recurring retainer packages, provided source records are delivered on time.

Can you help with cash-flow problems?

We analyze historical receipts, payments, receivables and supplier terms to identify pressure points and establish cash-flow forecasting models to optimize working capital.

Do you provide statutory audits?

Statutory audits must be performed by independent registered Chartered Accountants. We prepare clean, audit-ready accounting files and schedules to streamline statutory audits.
