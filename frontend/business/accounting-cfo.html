Accounting FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding software support, data security and virtual CFO engagement.

Which accounting software do you support?

We work extensively with Tally Prime, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Busy, and customized enterprise ERP setups. We also assist with smooth migration from legacy spreadsheets to cloud systems.

How does Virtual CFO work?

A Virtual CFO provides the strategic expertise of a full-time Chief Financial Officer on a fractional, flexible model. We attend your monthly reviews, analyze unit economics, and manage lender relationships.

Can you clear backlog accounting?

Yes. If your books are behind by several months or years, our team performs dedicated catch-up bookkeeping to reconcile your past transactions and prepare for overdue tax filings.

Is my company's financial data secure?

Yes. We operate under strict non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and use secure cloud-encrypted storage with role-based access controls to safeguard your financial privacy.
