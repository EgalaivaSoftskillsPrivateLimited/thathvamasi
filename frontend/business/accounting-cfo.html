Client Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our accounting and virtual CFO engagements.

Is virtual CFO service the same as bookkeeping?

No. Bookkeeping focuses on recording financial transactions. Virtual CFO support focuses on financial interpretation, forecasting, budgeting, cash-flow strategy and management decision-making.

Can I receive monthly financial reports?

Yes. Monthly management reports (MIS, P&L, balance sheet) can be included in an agreed service package, provided the required source records are supplied on time.

Can you help with cash-flow problems?

We can analyse available financial information, identify potential pressure points and help prepare forecasts and management actions. Outcomes depend on the business and its operational decisions.

Do you provide statutory audits?

Statutory audit engagements must be handled by appropriately qualified and authorised professionals under applicable law. Audit preparation and coordination can be discussed separately.
